November 5, 2026 - A site, finally

I have owned this domain for a while and pointed it at nothing, which is the most honest website I have ever had. This one is not much more than that, on purpose. I build things, I would rather be building things than maintaining a website, so the website is a text file with delusions of grandeur.

Short version of what is going on: there are thirteen modules live in the Voltage Modular store under BenDavis.IO, more in the approval queue, a sequencer family in the works that I care about more than is probably reasonable, and a plugin called Tern that just went out to friends as a beta. Records are on the music page. Hardware is on the about page.

If you bought a module and something is wrong with it, tell me. I would rather fix it than not know.

What this site will not do

No newsletter signup. No popups. No autoplay anything. If I write something new it will just be here, dated, like this. Check back the way people used to: whenever you remember to.
