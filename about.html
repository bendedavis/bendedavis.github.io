About

I'm Ben Davis. I design synthesizer modules in Fort Wayne, Indiana.

The short history: I came up through Eurorack hardware. The sequencer ideas people know as the Varigate and the Voltage Block came out of my work at Malekko Heavy Industry. I designed the original µBraids and kept my name off it. At 4ms I came up with the META module concept and wrote its VCV code. Those companies told those stories their way, or didn't. This site is mine, so here it is plainly.

These days the work is BenDavis.IO: Voltage Modular modules, the Tern plugin, and hardware of my own, including a guitar front-end pedal I have been refining for longer than I will admit in writing.

The through-line, if there is one: ideas I originated, built under my own name this time.
